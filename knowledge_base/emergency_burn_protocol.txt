SOURCE: American College of Surgeons. ATLS: Advanced Trauma Life Support. 10th ed. 2018.
SOURCE: Australian and New Zealand Burn Association. Emergency Management of Severe Burns 
(EMSB) Course Manual. 18th ed. 2016.
TOPIC: Emergency Room Protocol for Severe Burns

FIRST HOUR IN ER - STEP BY STEP:

STEP 1: PRIMARY SURVEY (0-5 min)
- Airway: Check for inhalation signs (singed nasal hair, hoarse voice, stridor)
- Breathing: O2 saturation, respiratory rate, chest wall compliance
- Circulation: 2 large bore IVs (avoid burned areas if possible)
- Start fluid if TBSA > 15% adults / > 10% children
- Disability: GCS, pupils
- Exposure: Remove ALL clothing and jewelry, estimate burn size

STEP 2: CALCULATE TBSA (5-10 min)
- Use Lund-Browder chart (age-adjusted) for accuracy
- Rule of Nines acceptable for rapid triage
- Do NOT include first-degree burns in calculation
- Document location, depth estimate, and percentage of each area

STEP 3: CALCULATE FLUIDS (10-15 min)
- Apply Parkland formula: 4 mL x kg x TBSA%
- Calculate rate for first 8 hours (from TIME OF BURN)
- Set up fluid monitoring, insert urinary catheter
- Target urine output: 0.5 mL/kg/hr adults / 1 mL/kg/hr children

STEP 4: ANALGESIA
- IV morphine 0.1 mg/kg titrated to pain score
- Consider ketamine 0.3-0.5 mg/kg for painful procedures
- Do NOT use IM route (unreliable absorption in burn patients)

STEP 5: WOUND CARE (after stabilization)
- Cool wounds only if < 3 hours since injury
- Gentle cleansing with chlorhexidine solution
- Silver sulfadiazine 1% cream or petroleum-impregnated gauze
- Elevate affected extremities

STEP 6: SECONDARY SURVEY
- Full head-to-toe examination
- Assess for associated trauma
- Circumferential burns -> monitor for compartment syndrome
- Chest/abdominal circumferential -> restrict ventilation -> escharotomy?

STEP 7: DISPOSITION DECISION
Admit to burn unit if:
- TBSA >= 10% in adults
- TBSA >= 5% in children or elderly
- Any full thickness burn
- Burns to face, hands, feet, genitalia
- Inhalation injury

Transfer to tertiary burn center if:
- TBSA > 20%
- Electrical or chemical burns
- Burns + trauma
- Pediatric burns > 10% TBSA

DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED:
- Time of burn injury (for fluid calculation)
- TBSA estimate by Lund-Browder
- Fluid administered pre-hospital
- Urine output since catheter insertion
- Pain scores before and after analgesia
- Tetanus status
