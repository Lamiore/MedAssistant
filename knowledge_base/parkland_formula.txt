---
SOURCE: Baxter CR, Shires T. Physiological response to crystalloid resuscitation of severe burns. 
Ann N Y Acad Sci. 1968;150(3):874-894.
TOPIC: Parkland Formula for Burn Fluid Resuscitation

FORMULA:
Total fluid in first 24 hours = 4 mL x Body Weight (kg) x TBSA (%)
Fluid type: Lactated Ringer's solution (Ringer's Lactate)

ADMINISTRATION SCHEDULE:
- First 8 hours from TIME OF BURN (not time of admission): Give 50% of total
- Next 16 hours: Give remaining 50%
IMPORTANT: Time is calculated from time of burn injury, NOT time of hospital arrival.
If patient arrives 2 hours after burn, the first 8-hour dose must be given in only 6 hours.

EXAMPLE:
Patient: 60 kg, TBSA = 30%
Total = 4 x 60 x 30 = 7,200 mL
First 8 hours: 3,600 mL -> rate = 450 mL/hour
Next 16 hours: 3,600 mL -> rate = 225 mL/hour

PEDIATRIC CONSIDERATION:
For children, add maintenance fluid (D5LR):
- 100 mL/kg for first 10 kg body weight
- 50 mL/kg for next 10 kg
- 20 mL/kg for each kg above 20 kg

MONITORING PARAMETERS:
- Urine output: Adults 0.5 mL/kg/hr | Children 1 mL/kg/hr
- Adjust rate based on urine output response
- Monitor for signs of over-resuscitation (pulmonary edema, abdominal compartment syndrome)

LIMITATIONS:
- Formula provides initial estimate only
- Must be titrated to clinical response
- Not suitable for chemical burns, electrical burns, or inhalation injury without modification

ALTERNATIVE FORMULAS:
- Modified Brooke: 2 mL x kg x TBSA% (uses less fluid, currently preferred by some centers)
- Galveston (pediatric): 5000 mL/m2 burned + 2000 mL/m2 BSA/day
